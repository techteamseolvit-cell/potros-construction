Our story

The Potros Family Story

Success isn’t just measured by what we create, but by the trust we earn along the way.

Potros Construction began as a family business with a simple idea: build outdoor spaces the way we would want them built at our own homes. That means honest conversations, careful craftsmanship and a finished project that holds up season after season.

Today we design and build hardscapes and outdoor living spaces for homeowners across Winchester and the surrounding communities. We are still hands-on with every job, and we still treat every client like a neighbour.
