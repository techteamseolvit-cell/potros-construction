Custom Patio Installations in Winchester VA

Our custom patio installations in Winchester VA are designed to transform your outdoor space into a stylish, functional, and comfortable living area.

Potros Construction specializes in building beautiful patios that enhance your home’s architecture while giving you a perfect place for relaxing, dining, or entertaining. We offer a wide range of high-quality materials—such as pavers, stamped concrete, brick, natural stone, and flagstone—to match your personal style and long-term durability needs. Every patio is built on a strong foundation with proper grading and drainage to prevent settling or water pooling. From small backyard sitting areas to large, multi-zone outdoor living spaces, we design each project with your lifestyle in mind. 03Built to last

Built With Strength, Longevity, and Weather Resistance

Our patios in Winchester VA are built to withstand heavy use, fluctuating temperatures, and year-round weather conditions. Potros Construction uses industry-leading techniques to ensure your patio remains stable, level, and durable.

We begin with a reinforced base, proper compaction, and precise leveling to eliminate future shifting or sinking. Materials are chosen for their strength, moisture resistance, and ability to maintain color over time. We also install joint sand, edge restraints, and drainage solutions to protect your patio from erosion and water damage. With our meticulous construction process, your patio stays beautiful and functional for years with minimal maintenance. Quality craftsmanship and premium materials ensure long-term performance you can rely on.

  • Reinforced base with proper compaction
  • Precise leveling to prevent shifting or sinking
  • Materials chosen for strength and moisture resistance
