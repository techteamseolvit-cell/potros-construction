Custom Pools in Winchester VA

A pool changes how a family uses its yard. The right design puts the pool, the deck and the seating areas together so the whole space works for swimming, relaxing and entertaining.

Potros Construction plans every pool project around your property, with careful attention to grading, drainage, safe surfaces and materials that hold up to sun, water and Virginia winters.

01Custom pools

Pools Designed Around Your Property and Your Family

Every pool starts with a visit to your property to understand the slope, the soil, the views and how you want to use the space. We help you choose the shape, size and finishes, and show you the result in 3D before any work begins.

The pool is designed together with its deck, walls and steps, so everything feels like one cohesive outdoor room.

  • Pool design planned around your yard
  • Grading and drainage engineered first
  • 3D virtual design before we build

02Pool decks

Pool Decks and Surrounds Built for Comfort and Safety

The surface around a pool takes constant wear from water, sun and foot traffic. We build pool decks and surrounds in natural flagstone, pavers and other durable materials, with a stable base and proper grading so water drains away from the pool and your home.

Every layout is planned around how you use the space, with room for lounging, dining and walking comfortably.

  • Flagstone and paver pool decks
  • Proper grading and drainage
  • Surfaces chosen for comfort and durability

03Walls & lighting

Stone Walls, Lighting and Outdoor Living Around the Pool

Stone walls frame the pool, support level areas on sloped yards and create built-in seating. Combined with columns, caps and lighting, they give the space structure and a finished, custom look after the sun goes down.

We can also add fire pits, fireplaces and outdoor kitchens so the pool area works for every season.

  • Natural stone retaining and seating walls
  • Wall, column and step lighting
  • Fire pits, fireplaces and outdoor kitchens
