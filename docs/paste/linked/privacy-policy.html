Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions

Who We Are

Our website address is: https://potrosconstruction.com.

Comments

When visitors leave comments on our website, we collect the information provided in the comments form, the visitor’s IP address, and browser user-agent data to assist with spam detection.

An anonymized string created from your email address (also known as a hash) may be provided to the Gravatar service to determine if you are using it. The Gravatar privacy policy can be viewed at https://automattic.com/privacy/. Once your comment is approved, your profile picture will be visible publicly in the context of your comment.

Media Uploads

If you upload images to the website, please avoid including images with embedded location data (EXIF GPS). Visitors to our website can download and extract any location data contained within images published on the site.

Cookies

If you leave a comment, you have the option to save your name, email address, and website details in cookies. These cookies enhance your convenience, allowing your details to auto-fill when you comment again. These cookies remain active for one year.

When accessing our login page, a temporary cookie is set to verify if your browser accepts cookies. This cookie contains no personal data and is deleted when you close your browser.

Upon logging in, we set several cookies to save your login credentials and your screen display preferences. Login cookies remain active for two days, while screen option cookies last one year. Selecting the “Remember Me” option extends your login persistence to two weeks. Logging out will clear these login cookies.

If you edit or publish an article, an additional cookie is stored in your browser, containing no personal data but simply noting the post ID of the article edited. This cookie expires after one day.

Embedded Content From External Sites

Articles published on our website may contain embedded content such as videos, images, and articles from external websites. Embedded content behaves exactly as if the visitor had visited the external website directly.

These external websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed third-party tracking mechanisms, and monitor your interactions with the embedded content. This includes tracking interactions if you are logged into an account on that external website.

Microsoft Clarity

We partner with Microsoft Clarity and Microsoft Advertising to capture how you use and interact with our website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay to improve user experience. Website usage data is captured using first and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies to determine the popularity of pages/products and online activity. Additionally, we use this information for site optimization, fraud/security purposes, and advertising. For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses your data, please visit the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Sharing Your Data

If you request a password reset, your IP address will be included in the reset email sent to you.

Data Retention

Comments and their associated metadata are retained indefinitely. This enables us to recognize and approve follow-up comments automatically without placing them into moderation queues.

For registered users (if any), we store the personal details provided within user profiles. Users can view, edit, or delete their personal information at any time (with the exception of usernames, which cannot be changed). Website administrators can also view and edit this information.

Your Rights Regarding Your Data

If you have an account or have left comments on our website, you have the right to request an export file containing the personal data we hold about you, including any data you provided to us. You may also request the deletion of your personal data. This does not include data we are required to retain for administrative, legal, or security purposes.

Where Your Data is Sent

Visitor comments may be processed through an automated spam detection service.
