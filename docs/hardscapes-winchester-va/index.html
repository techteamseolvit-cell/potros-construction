Hardscapes in Winchester VA Designed for Beauty and Durability

Hardscaping is the structural framework of your landscape: the patios, walkways, retaining walls, stone borders and masonry that give an outdoor space its shape and make it usable every day.

Our hardscaping services in Winchester VA are built to enhance your property with long-lasting, functional, and beautiful outdoor features. Every project begins with a consultation, and we build with premium materials and careful attention to grading, drainage and structure.

01Patios

Custom Patios Built to Transform Your Outdoor Space

A custom patio is one of the most valuable upgrades you can make to your outdoor area. We design each patio for proper drainage and long-term stability, and around the way you actually use the space: dining, lounging, grilling and entertaining.

  • Paver and natural stone patios
  • Dining, lounging and grilling zones
  • Drainage and a stable base engineered first

02Walkways

Walkways and Paths That Improve Flow and Functionality

Walkways are essential for creating a safe and welcoming outdoor environment. We build paths in brick, pavers, stone, concrete or gravel, with proper grading and a stable base so they stay level and safe through every season.

  • Brick, paver, stone, concrete and gravel paths
  • Steps and landings that connect home and yard
  • Clean borders and consistent grading

03Retaining walls

Retaining Walls for Support, Strength, and Landscape Appeal

Retaining walls are a powerful addition to any yard, offering structural support while adding style. We build them in stone, concrete block or segmental systems for erosion control, planting beds and curb appeal.

  • Natural stone and segmental block walls
  • Erosion control and level terraces
  • Columns, caps and integrated lighting
